In short: Backroad Rumble does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking service and no server of ours. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

Game progress, best times, settings, the optional player name and which packs you bought are stored locally in the app only. We have no access to them. Deleting the app deletes this data. It may be part of the device backup you set up with Apple or Google yourself.

Playing on Wi-Fi

In multiplayer mode, devices on the same local network connect directly to each other. Only game data is exchanged between the participating devices (player name, car, positions in the race). None of it is sent to the internet or to us. On iPhone and iPad the system asks for the “Local Network” permission; without it, only Wi-Fi play is unavailable.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple’s App Store or Google Play. Payment and account data are processed only by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we receive no information about you. The app checks the purchase status through the store interface on your device to unlock the packs you bought.

No sharing, no advertising

No data is shared with third parties. The app contains no advertising, no trackers and no usage analytics libraries. Microphone, camera, photos, contacts and location are not used.

Children

As the app collects no personal data, no data is collected from children either.

Contact and your rights

For privacy questions, write to erdwind@live.de. If you send us an email, we use your address and message only to reply and delete them when they are no longer needed. Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and the GDPR you have the right to access, correction and deletion.

Changes

If anything about the app changes that affects privacy, we will update this page. The version published here applies.
